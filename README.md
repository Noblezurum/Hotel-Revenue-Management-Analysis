# Hotel-Revenue-Management-Analysis
ey Revenue Management Insights
1. Average Daily Rate (ADR) by Market Segment
Online Booking Dominance: The Online channel accounts for the highest average daily rate ($112.26), followed by Aviation ($100.70), Offline ($91.63), and Corporate ($82.91).

Trade-off: While Online yields the highest ADR, it also suffers from the highest cancellation rate (36.5%), compared to Corporate bookings which have a low 10.9% cancellation rate.

2. Revenue at Risk Analysis
Out of $11.35 Million in total potential gross bookings, $4.30 Million (37.9%) was uncollected due to cancellations.

The main drivers of revenue loss are long lead times and Online Travel Agency (OTA) bookings with flexible cancellation policies.

3. Price Elasticity & Demand Dynamics
Price vs. Cancellation Risk: As room rates increase, cancellation rates climb significantly:

Very Low Tier (~$67/night): 21.5% cancellation rate.

High Tier (~$117/night): 44.5% cancellation rate.

Elasticity Ceiling: Beyond the $117/night threshold, price sensitivity increases dramatically, causing elevated cancellation rates unless paired with non-refundable deposit terms.

4. Lead Time Dynamics
Bookings made 0–30 days before arrival have a low 15.2% cancellation rate.

Bookings made 181–360 days in advance suffer a 72.8% cancellation rate, escalating to over 90% for bookings made over a year in advance.

Strategic Revenue Optimization Recommendations
Implement Dynamic Deposit & Cancellation Policies:

Require non-refundable deposits for bookings made with lead times greater than 90 days or room rates above $115/night.

Optimize Segment Mix:

Increase allocation for Corporate segments, which offer steady revenue and low cancellation rates (10.9%), even if ADR is lower.

Overbooking & Capacity Management:

Build overbooking models targeting high-ADR Online bookings made >90 days in advance to mitigate the ~40% expected cancellation loss.
